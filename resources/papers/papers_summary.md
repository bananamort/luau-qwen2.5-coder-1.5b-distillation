# Research Papers & Technical Foundations

This directory contains full-text, responsive HTML versions of the foundational research papers supporting the Luau Qwen knowledge distillation, fine-tuning, and serving pipeline.

---

| **[`2409.12186_Qwen2.5_Coder_Technical_Report.html`](file:///Users/jasonhuang/Desktop/model/resources/papers/2409.12186_Qwen2.5_Coder_Technical_Report.html)** | *Qwen2.5-Coder Technical Report*<br>(Binyuan Hui, Jian Yang, Zeyu Cui, Dayiheng Liu, et al.) | 2024<br>[arXiv:2409.12186](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12186) | Technical specifications of the `Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B` base student: 5.5T pre-training tokens, 151,936 shared BPE vocabulary, RoPE YaRN support, and 28-layer transformer layout. |
